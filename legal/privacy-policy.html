Privacy Policy



At Brand Point, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our digital printing and offset printing services. By using our website and services, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide you with the best possible service. The types of information we may collect include:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing/shipping address.
  • Payment Information: Credit card or other payment method details (processed securely through third-party payment processors).
  • Order Information: Details of your print projects, including design files, specifications, and quantity orders.
  • Website Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, such as IP addresses, browser type, and browsing activity (collected through cookies and other tracking technologies).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing Orders: To fulfill your printing orders, including custom digital and offset printing services.
  • Customer Service: To communicate with you about your orders, provide support, and respond to inquiries.
  • Marketing Communications: To send you promotional materials, offers, and updates related to our services (you can opt-out at any time).
  • Improving Our Website and Services: To enhance your user experience on our website and continuously improve our printing solutions.

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include encryption, secure payment gateways, and access controls within our systems.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. However, we may share your information in the following situations:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who help us fulfill your orders (e.g., payment processors, shipping carriers, and IT services).
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Brand Point or our customers, we may disclose your information.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small files placed on your device that help us remember your preferences, analyze site traffic, and improve functionality. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights and choices regarding your personal information:

  • Access and Correction: You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, as well as to update or correct any inaccuracies.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing Communications: You can opt-out of receiving marketing emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails or contacting us directly.
  • Data Deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and operational obligations.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites that are not operated by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
